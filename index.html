NRCEP EXPLAINED

What is NRCEP?

NRCEP refers to the National Registry of Certified Environmental Professionals. It is associated with Malaysia's environmental competency certification framework and provides an online point of access for relevant Environmental Competent Person information and services.

If you are trying to access your NRCEP account, check competency-related information, or find CPD resources, the safest approach is to start from the official NRCEP portal and official DOE/EiMAS publications.

Official NRCEP portal

Use the government website below for actual account access and official services.

nrcep.doe.gov.my →

How to access NRCEP

  1. Open the official NRCEP login page.
  2. Sign in using your registered account credentials.
  3. Use the relevant account or competency functions available to you.
  4. For official requirements or changes to the system, rely on DOE/EiMAS announcements and documents.

Can't find NRCEP on Google?

If you previously found NRCEP through Google but it no longer appears in normal search results, try entering the official domain directly in your browser:

https://nrcep.doe.gov.my/

You can also bookmark the official login page. Search visibility can change over time and does not necessarily mean that the government system is offline.

NRCEP CPD

Continuing Professional Development (CPD) is an important part of maintaining professional competency. The exact requirements, acceptable activities, supporting documents and submission procedures should be checked against the latest official DOE/EiMAS guidance because requirements and procedures can change.

For official CPD-related guidance, use the DOE/EiMAS resources rather than relying on an unofficial summary.

Environmental Competent Person resources

NRCEP is relevant to Malaysia's Environmental Competent Person ecosystem. Different competency areas have their own technical requirements and training pathways. Examples commonly encountered in environmental work include wastewater treatment, scheduled waste and related environmental competency programmes.

For current course schedules, eligibility requirements and official competency information, check EiMAS and DOE directly.

NRCEP login problems

If the login page is unavailable or you cannot access your account, avoid entering your credentials into third-party pages. Go directly to the official NRCEP domain.

  • Check that you are using the official nrcep.doe.gov.my domain.
  • Try a current browser and clear cached site data if the page behaves unexpectedly.
  • Check official DOE/EiMAS announcements for system maintenance.
  • For account-specific issues, contact the responsible NRCEP/DOE support channel.

Frequently asked questions

Is this the official NRCEP website?

No. This website is an independent guide. The official NRCEP portal is hosted at nrcep.doe.gov.my.

Where should I log in to NRCEP?

Where can I find CPD information?

No. This site only provides information and links. Account actions and official services should be performed on the government NRCEP portal.
